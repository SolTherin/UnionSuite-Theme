This is the one that can misfire. "Before the CRM existed" in practice means "before the migration cut-off", and migrations are where join dates go to die. A record defaulted to 01/01/1900, or a blank coerced to epoch, would earn this marker — on exactly the records staff trust least. Gate it on a plausible date window (see the floor below), and consider whether the honest version is a data-quality flag wearing a joke.
