Error pages — the tone ladder

The idea was a lost forklift wandering an empty warehouse. The thing worth testing here is not the forklift, it is the ladder: how much charm each severity level can carry before it starts working against the user.

Rule applied throughout — the error text is primary and fully legible, and the art sits below it. A user hitting an error needs to know what broke and what to do next.

Design note. Deliberately empty. A permission refusal can be a sensitive moment — the user may have been told they had access, or may be about to ask a colleague why they don't. Any illustration here reads as the system being pleased with itself. The absence is the design decision worth reviewing.
