4 open ← bulk action: no celebration, by design

Design note — the tiers are about proportion, not volume. If a two-day task got confetti, the 412-day one would have nowhere left to go. The ordinary case has to stay almost silent for the rare case to mean anything, which is why tier 1 is a tick and a ring that lasts half a second.

What the copy is carefully not saying

"Opened 412 days ago" reads two ways: well done, you cleared something ancient, or this organisation ignored a member for over a year. The person closing it is very often not the person who let it sit.

Works

"Opened 412 days ago. Closed today."

Two facts, no verdict.

Doesn't

"Finally! Only took 412 days."

Now it is about the delay, and the delay has an owner.

The number also stays out of reports, dashboard tiles and exports. In a toast it is a moment; in a column header it is a metric someone gets asked about in a team meeting.
