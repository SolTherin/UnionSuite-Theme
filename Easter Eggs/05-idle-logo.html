The logo that looks at you, then falls asleep

Two ideas from the register built as one component with two states, which is the point: a mark that blinks and a mark that tracks the cursor are the same mark, and designing them separately means they fight over the same eyes.

Move your pointer anywhere on this page. Stop moving it for the idle delay below and the mascot dozes off. Touch it, click, type, scroll — it wakes instantly.

The mascot is a membership card with a face — the product's own object rather than a borrowed animal. It gives F4 (the long-press hand-drawn swap) something to swap to, and it gives the 31 October ghost somewhere to live.

Design note — the asymmetry is the whole thing. Closing takes 1.5 seconds; waking takes 120 milliseconds. A logo that is even a fifth of a second slow to wake does not read as sleepy, it reads as a frozen page — and a staff member who thinks the CRM has hung will start clicking things. Slow out, instant in, always.

The size test is the real test. At 34px the mouth and the card stripe stop reading and the eyes do all the work — which is why the eyes are oversized relative to a realistic card. If a mark's eyes disappear in the taskbar, it cannot carry either of these eggs and the idea dies at the art stage, not the code stage.

Constraints this mockup implements

  • Throttled through requestAnimationFrame. One transform write per frame at most, no matter how fast the pointer moves. Listeners are passive.
  • Paused when the tab is hidden. Watch the "tab" readout — switch tabs and come back. No animation frames are requested in the background, and the idle clock does not accrue while nobody is looking.
  • Off on touch. There is no cursor to follow, and a pointer event from a finger would make the eyes jump to wherever the last tap was and freeze there.
  • The idle timer does not touch the session. It is a local setTimeout that renders eyelids. It must never be mistaken for, or wired into, the iMIS session-timeout warning — those are the user's actual session and this is a cartoon.
  • Reduced motion. Transitions drop out entirely; the mascot still opens and closes its eyes, it just does it instantly. The egg degrades to a state change rather than disappearing.
