1. Current theme (us-contact)

The agreement page today: one full-width row per contact. Name, role badge and edit, then type and group badges, then email and phone.

2. Original (v1 mock-up)

The v1 contacts card in its own styling, always light. A grid of cards: coloured avatar, name, role, type and group badges, then phone and email.

3. Grouped roster (us-contacts-roster)

A directory for long lists. Contacts sit under their group with a count. One row each, with columns that line up: avatar tinted by type, name and role, type, email, phone, edit. Below about 620px, email and phone drop under the name. Needs the IQA sorted by group.

Contacts

4. Quiet tiles (us-contacts-tiles)

The v1 look, calmer. Each tile has an avatar tinted by type, the name, and one muted "role · group" line. There are no badges. Email, phone and edit are icon buttons, and the address or number shows on hover. The IQA is unchanged.

Contacts

5. Filterable directory (us-contacts-facets)

The roster rows with a filter strip over them: one chip per group and one per role, each with a count. Press a chip to see everyone in that group or with that role; a group and a role combine, and the other facet's counts follow. Roles past the first six sit behind "more". The strip stays put while the list scrolls, and does not appear at all on an agreement with four contacts or fewer. The theme's search still works on top. Groups and roles come from the data, so nothing is hard-coded. IQA sorted by group, as in option 3.

Contacts

6. Tiles with a group filter (us-contacts-tiles + us-contacts-group-filter)

A short card per contact: name with the type (system: Staff, Member, Delegate, External) under it; then, left aligned under the avatar, role · group (the person's place on this agreement), wrapping instead of clipping; then email and phone as plain text, each always in the same place with its icon (no mailto: or tel: links; email goes through iMIS so it is tracked). The group quick filter sits behind the funnel with the search, groups only, one chip per group with a count. Typing in the search lists matching names, roles, groups and types, each labelled by attribute as v1 did; pick one to filter on that attribute exactly. The count sits beside the title and reads "4 of 15 · Employer · ROLE Consultant" with a clear while filters are on; the funnel gets a dot. The send button in the heading is the placeholder for the email contacts action (it opens the Email negotiating team page until the destination is decided; without an AgreementID in the URL it stays disabled here). The avatar is tinted by group, not type: groups take the theme's hues in IQA order (teal, purple, green, amber) and a blank group stays grey. Chips appear only with more than four contacts.

7. Grouped tiles (us-agreement-contacts)

Option 6 with the review's recommendations. Group headings over the tiles, each with its colour dot and count, hidden while a group filter is on or when the list is small. Group colours come from the IQA (ContactGroupTone), so a group keeps its colour on every agreement. The Lead is first in their group, with a solid initials badge, a crown and a one-off sheen. The name opens the contact's record (Ben Okafor has no link, so his stays plain). With a filter on, the send button emails only the contacts on screen. Search suggests names, roles and groups, not the system type. No list scrolling: the tab scrolls as a page. The whole class list is one preset class.
