3. Rows (us-terms)

No cards: one row per term under a rule. The term, then its status badge and amount on one line, then its description; the category and the pencil each in a fixed column, so they line up down the list. Status quick filters sit in the funnel disclosure, as the contacts panel's group chips do (a pressed chip puts a dot on the funnel and the count beside the title). The term column stops at 640px, so on a wide tab the category stays beside the term; the pencil keeps the right edge under Add term, and the spare width sits inside the row between them (options 4 to 6 too). The amount shows its unit. IQA sorted by term.

7. Expandable rows (us-terms us-terms--expand) — decided, now in the theme

Option 4's look, closed to three facts: the status icon in its own column, the term with its status and amount on the line under it ("Included · 17.5%", as option 4), and the category in its column, with a chevron. Click the icon to change the status from a menu; changing to Included plays the confetti. Terms have no status save yet, so this page stands in a pretend one (the toolbar's "Status save" makes it fail, to see the change revert). Click the row (or press Enter on it) and it opens, as the contact page's activity history cards do, to the description and the actions: View, Edit and Delete at the bottom right. Click anywhere on an open row to close it. View and Delete have no actions defined yet, so they do nothing here. Own template: Agreement-Terms-Expand-Query-Template.html. The first row is opened here so the open state shows.
