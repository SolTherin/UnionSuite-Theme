1. Current (v0.1)

The live Terms & Schedule tab, rebuilt as static markup in the theme, filled from the sample as v0.1's three IQAs would show it. A summary Query Menu (Class, Total Increase), a form, and All Increases: a General table and a Class table, each with Export, the Class table with a Class filter. The summary mixes units: General is a sum of percentages, Fixed a sum of dollars, both in one column. A CPI increase shows as a blank amount. A fixed-amount general increase appears only in the summary (the General list filters Type = General), and a class step in dollars cannot be entered (the form takes a percentage), so it shows blank here. The layout v2 page (index-v2.html) already keeps only the summary table, with the form moved to the Add increase heading button.

2. Summary + breakdown (two iParts, two IQAs)

A compact summary panel from a summary IQA: General first (what everyone gets), then one line per class with that class's own steps, each with its total; the IQA sums, so the totals need no script. The units and "+ CPI" are drawn by the CSS from which sums are blank. Under it, the breakdown: every increase on the rows IQA, grouped General then each class, the headings counting only (the totals are above). Two iParts to place and two IQAs to keep in step; the summary IQA cannot compound, and prints its sums as the IQA formats them (6.50, 4788.00). Add increase sits on the summary only.

3. One grouped list (us-increases-c us-increases--grouped us-increases--totals)

One IQA, one iPart. A strip at the top answers "what is the total": what everyone gets, how many classes and steps (and the largest class total), and when the general increases take effect. Then every increase, under a heading per group whose total sits in the amount column, so General's 6.5% + CPI + $40.00 lines up with each row's amount. Rows: the type icon in its column, when it applies with the type coloured under it, the amount right-aligned in a fixed column, the pencil at the right edge under Add increase. Everything is visible without a click; at about 50px a row it suits up to 15 or 20 increases.

4. Expandable groups (us-agreement-increases) — decided, now in the theme

The locked terms look, one row per group: the group icon in its own column, the group with a coloured line under it (Everyone · 4 increases, 1 Jul 2026 to 1 Jul 2028; Class · 2 steps, after 6 to 12 months), its total in a fixed column, a chevron. The closed panel is the summary; click a row (or Enter on it) and its increases slide in under the title, each amount under the group total and each pencil under the chevron. Click anywhere on an open group to close it. The same totals strip leads. Same IQA and template as option 3; the groups are built by script from the rows, so without it the rows show flat. General is opened here so the open state shows.

5. Class matrix (us-increases-c us-increases--matrix)

General increases as a dated strip with everyone's total; then a table with a row per class and a column per step (6, 12, 24 months in class), with a total column, so classes compare side by side. Same IQA and template; the table is pivoted by script from the rows, which is the catch: without the script there is no table, the month columns grow with every distinct step, and a cell has no room for the pencil, so editing needs another route. A narrow panel scrolls the table sideways with the class column fixed.
