1. Fold away (baseline)

The theme's usual motion and nothing more: the row slides aside as it fades, then the gap closes. About 0.4 seconds.

2. Black hole

The bin's lid lifts and a swirl opens behind it. The row leans away for a moment, then spirals in, shrinking and blurring, and the bin swallows. About 1.5 seconds.

3. Crumple and toss

The row scrunches into a paper ball, which arcs up and drops into the bin; the lid snaps shut and the bin rocks. About 1.7 seconds.

4. Shredder

The bin rattles, the row is fed in and cut into ten strips, and the strips tumble into the bin one after another. About 1.6 seconds.

5. Word vacuum

The bin breathes in and the row's words lift off and fly into it one at a time, nearest first, as the row fades behind them. Longer rows take a little longer, up to about 1.5 seconds.
