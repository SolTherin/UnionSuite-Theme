Decided (owner, 4 October 2026): one card per send, shown by the kind of send

The contact page's activity feed and record card, unchanged. Each kind of send is its own source, filtered on the tag the send hook gives it, so the card's type line says who it went to (the group, or Members) and opening it shows how it went:

  • Group send (a contact group such as SBU, 10 to 15 people): the summary bar and counts, then every recipient with their status, failures first. Past 15, "Show all" slides in the rest. Open the SBU or Bargaining Team cards.
  • Member send (every covered member, possibly thousands): the summary bar and counts only, from one row of totals; the send's own record lists the people. Open "Bargaining update #5".
  • SMS works the same way on the SMS card (no Opened state). Incoming email comes from the Outlook integration, on the same card. Every card marks its direction as Incoming (down arrow) or Outgoing (up arrow).
