2. Decided (owner, 4 October 2026), in the theme: rule groups (us-agreement-coverage)

Each rank becomes a tinted box labelled with its real rank, "Rank 2 · all must match" ("all active rules must match" when one is disabled), with its rules inside joined by "and"; an "or" sits between the boxes. This is how the CA: Update Covered Contacts flow applies them: every enabled rule in a rank must match, and any rank covers. Each rule reads left to right: the record in everyday words (Workplace, Member, Job, Member profile, Employer, for the CloudToolz tables in Rule1), then the field, the condition (is, is not) and the value, with the workplace's name when the IQA returns one. Every rule ends with a plain sign, a green Enabled or a red Disabled (no badge); a disabled rule stays in its rank, lightly struck through. Hover a rule to see which sign is its own. Update coverage opens the existing popup. The covered counts are a Needs Attention tracker above the rules on the agreement page (index-v2.html; owner, 4 October 2026).
