Milestones

6. In the theme

  • Milestones are undated lifecycle stages; the bar shows progress, the line under it the next stage.
  • The status icon opens the status menu; a completed stage slides out. The pencil opens the stage.
  • The toggle in the heading reorders them: drag a handle, or focus one and use the arrow keys.

7. With tasks linked to each stage (planned)

  • Each stage says how far its tasks have got, and how many are overdue; the in-progress segment of the bar fills with them.
  • Click a stage to open its tasks; tick them there. Done tasks group after the open ones.
  • Tick the last open task and the stage offers to complete itself; the status menu warns when tasks are still open.
  • Add a task at the foot of the list: type and press Enter. Each task’s pencil opens it in full.
