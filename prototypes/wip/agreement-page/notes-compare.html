4. Ledger rows (us-notes--ledger)

Proposed for long lists: no cards, one row per note under a rule. The date in its own column so dates line up, the author first, badges and a view icon at the right, the note underneath, capped at three lines with More to read the rest. The magnifier in the heading is the theme’s search filter (us-query-search). Needs one new IQA column, CreatedTime.
