Johnson v. Pacific Industries — Workplace Injury Dispute

Active

Workplace injury sustained on 14 March 2024 resulting in fractures to the left wrist and forearm. The claimant was operating a forklift when the load shifted unexpectedly. Liability is contested by the employer, who alleges that the required loading procedure was not followed. The case team is reviewing the incident report, available witness statements and medical documentation to establish the sequence of events and the impact of the injury. Further information is required about equipment maintenance, operator training and the supervision arrangements in place at the time. The primary complainant will be contacted to confirm outstanding details before the next case review.
